## Asset provenance

The four production Jim GLBs are exact copies of the approved files under art/jim/models. Accessory geometry is unchanged from the approved bundle. Promotion generated with OpenAI image tools using owner-supplied IMG_0706.jpeg, IMG_0703(3).jpeg and the approved outfit sheet. Final prompt called for a peaceful, non-combat coastal golf scene with both outfits, original-photo facial proportions, front-facing faces and distinct footwear. The combat revision was rejected by the image service; no combat revision was substituted through another service. The final source generation is exec-d24af36d-99f9-4d7f-99de-8b5ccb2a5eea.png, converted to public/season/jim-icon.webp. Promotional text describes game cosmetics, not separate real-product advertising.
