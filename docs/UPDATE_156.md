# Update 156 — Bring Your Crew

Version 5.10.0, protocol 52.

Spectators use Room chat by default. Social now has Friends and All Online, including friends, and reports live spectating correctly. The active browser tab takes priority for party launches and presence. Closing Emotes returns straight to gameplay.

Private Royale hosts can start directly from Spawn Island. The full warmup map previews the actual Kestrel route and Hot Drop, which remain unchanged at departure. The cargo door slides open through the three-second departure countdown. Bots dive and glide according to distance and remaining altitude; boss sentinels patrol separate posts throughout their district.

Invite & Earn supplies a personal referral link, a new recruitment briefing after the Season 1 screen, an independent Do not show again preference, and a permanent lobby button. Each new player who opens the link and chooses a name credits 1,000 Marks once. Referrals persist online, including offline rewards, and wallet synchronization prevents replay across tabs and reconnects.

Frags use a three-second fuse, 100 player damage, 375 structure damage, swept bounces, friction and blast-cover checks. Grenades and Smoke Canisters display a collision-aware aiming arc generated by their actual throw physics. Smoke canisters travel and bounce before releasing twenty seconds of animated turbulent smoke. These are original Fortnite-inspired rules, not a copy of proprietary engine constants.

ATVs display damage numbers and hit markers. Uplink information follows the minimap in the right HUD lane, clear of chat. Nyx’s protruding stair object is removed and its terrain entrance remains walkable. Victories retain two seconds of first-person slow motion before the Victory Royale screen. ## Validation

Focused behavior tests cover room chat, shared planning, guard spacing, landing decisions, throw-preview parity, bouncing/fuse timing, smoke deployment, vehicle feedback, persistent referral attribution and wallet replay protection, active-tab presence and delayed victory. Existing combat, vault traversal, snapshot, social, private-match and teammate suites remain in the release checks. Browser acceptance exercises a new referral, reconnect deduplication, host/guest departure, trajectory/smoke rendering, HUD clearance, spectator chat, emote close and victory reveal.

Epic’s public grenade documentation confirms the aiming arc and twenty-second smoke duration: https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/using-grenade-items-in-fortnite-creative . The frag uses classic-style tuning; restitution, drag and launch parameters are original approximations.

Publication is authorized without waiting for occupied games. Matching Pages and relay builds are checked after deployment.
