# Arena streaks

Every five consecutive arena eliminations awards one of six original field bonuses: Barrier (100 armor), Overdrive (temporary double damage), Resupply, Second Wind (decaying overhealth), Focus (20% lower spread) or Quickstep (12% movement increase). Timed effects last 15 seconds with bounded extensions. Death resets them. Royale does not use these bonuses. Every operator retains the same humanoid combat regions and dimensions. Streak bonuses never grant currency; the online relay handles match rewards.
