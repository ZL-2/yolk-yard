# Update 213 — A cleaner home screen

The home screen now uses one slate-and-gold visual system, consistent panel borders, spacing, typography and controls. Existing navigation, the left Play area, center operator and right party/rankings remain in familiar positions.

Referrals and feature requests move beneath the party. Chat and voice sit in the bottom bar, and Help & More groups player details, instructions, connection reporting and credits. This menu supports keyboard dismissal and closes after an action or outside click.

News and Play share a flexible left column. The party column and ranking viewport retain scrolling, including compact laptop windows and larger parties. Tournament cards stay in the center above the operator area.

Verification: actual browser at 1440×900, 1280×720 and 1024×600; panel clearance; chat; help; mode discovery; privacy/options; built-in navigation; tournament card clearance. Build and release-history validation. Version 6.21.1, protocol 81 unchanged; no gameplay or reward changes.
