# Update 173 — Blacktide Relay

Version 6.0.0 / protocol 59. Original Zombies expansion, server-authoritative one-to-four-player cooperative survival, isolated from the existing player-hosted PvP modes.

Research and the essential/optional/incompatible feature matrix are in [ZOMBIES_RESEARCH.md](ZOMBIES_RESEARCH.md). Numerical tuning is original and centralized in `src/zombies-config.js`.

## Content

Blacktide Relay: six connected sectors, seven purchasable routes, a raised gallery with two stairs, covered machinery halls and the Beacon Platform. Eight infected archetypes; six perks with a four-slot limit; five wall weapon locations; a weighted relocating Signal Cache; three resonance tiers; salvage calibration and ammunition mods; three original special weapons. Barricade repairs, traps, power-ups, armor, grenades, decoys and two charged field abilities support survival.

The optional Last Transmission quest restores power, recovers three cores, solves a recorded sequence, charges three combat conduits and summons the Bellwether. Three recordings unlock a secret weapon archive. A separate Freight Arcade timed trial awards a one-time team reward. Optional majority-voted extraction opens after round 10 and every fifth round.

## Multiplayer and authority

Social party reservations are atomic for one to four operators. Public runs fill by cooperative capacity; private rooms use invite codes. Joins during a wave wait for the next round; admission closes after round 10 and during extraction/results. A short transport reconnect preserves the same operator and run. Leaving leadership elects another operator without migrating simulation. Empty rooms are removed and reservations released.

The relay owns all AI, damage, health, currencies, station interactions, perks, quest progress, revives, extraction and rewards. Purchases verify range, line of sight, unlocked sector, funds and station state. Capped damage credit, one-time quest rewards, bounded repairs, per-run/per-hour Marks limits and contribution checks constrain farming. Zombies records never increment PvP eliminations or Royale wins.

## Performance design

Maximum 32 living infected and 10 short-lived corpses; four shared reverse navigation fields instead of individual path searches. Pooled enemies, bounded effects/drops/grenades, distance animation scheduling and instanced articulated geometry. Authoritative snapshots use entity deltas at 20 Hz with backpressure; cosmetic events contain a small allowlisted payload. Actual measurements and desktop evidence are generated by the focused acceptance workflow.

## Verification status

The focused CI suite passes 95 gameplay, real-socket multiplayer and regression checks. These cover public party admission, capacity, reconnects, leader departure, failed partner reservations, private starts, late joins, navigation/climbing, purchases, revives, the full quest, extraction, reward receipts and PvP isolation.

The ten-minute simulated four-player load run with 32 active infected measured a 0.831 ms simulation-step p99, 22.74 ms of CPU work per simulated second, 44.11 KiB/s of encoded snapshot traffic per player, and 25.41 MiB retained heap growth. Entity counts stayed within their configured caps. These are GitHub CI measurements, not a promise of zero latency or a hardware frame-rate benchmark.

Desktop rendered review remains required before publishing. The live release check will verify matching website/relay builds, a private PvP host/guest match and a separate authoritative private Zombies duo. No mobile additions or mobile acceptance runs are included.
