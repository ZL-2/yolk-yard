# Authoritative Marks — 6.35.0 / protocol 90

All spendable balances, purchases and credit deduplication are stored in SQLite by authenticated social identity. Client-supplied balances, earned counters and reward amounts are never imported. Shop quotes and entitlements are recomputed on the service and requests are idempotent. Match payouts have a shared 900/hour social-identity cap and a 600/match cap. Grants and tournament awards remain server-created.

Legacy migration uses server-recorded match rewards, owner/tournament grants and a frozen deployment-time snapshot of non-outlier historical referral awards (at most 25 referrals, excluding the audited identity). Claimed legacy cosmetics must fit that verified budget; unknown historical earnings are not silently trusted. Legacy wallets had no purchase timestamps, so an exact two-day purchase rollback cannot be reconstructed. New purchases have timestamped ledger entries.

New referral attribution has no immediate currency payout. Qualification requires an identity at least 24 hours old and three positive public match receipts, each with at least two participating human opponents, spanning at least 20 minutes. Awards are unique per invitee, with limits of three per rolling day and twenty per inviter lifetime. These controls bound rewards; browser identities do not establish a real-world person. Browser storage changes cannot alter the server ledger. Player-hosted simulation remains a trust limitation, so this is not a claim that all possible cheating is eliminated.

Private owner-directed account correction: authoritative balance 2,000; keep full Jim bundle; one-hour play suspension starting on first deployment of the correction; exclude next scheduled Cup only. The durable correction key prevents extending or reapplying it on restarts. Historical awards preceding the correction cannot refill the reset balance. Account notice explains evidence, correction, time restrictions and appeal; UI requires a complete scroll, reading interval and explicit acknowledgement, with the interval also enforced on the server. Software cannot prove actual human reading comprehension.

Validation: economy-authority.test.js covers fabricated migration values, duplicate credits/purchases, conflicting identities, negative prices, restricted cosmetics, referral qualification and deduplication, cross-token hourly limits and idempotent owner corrections. economy-browser-check.mjs exercises actual migration, purchases, reconnect correction and mandatory notice acknowledgement. The focused production workflow includes live frontend/relay protocol and storage checks.
