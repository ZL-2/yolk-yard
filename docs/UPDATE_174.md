# Update 174 — Box Fights

Box Fights is available from Choose Game Mode. Queue into 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 or 4v4, or create a private room with team selection and optional eight-player Free For All.

- Three enclosed arenas: The Foundry, Relay Chamber and Drydock. Rotation changes the arena every two rounds; teams swap sides each round.
- Seven-second protected build phase, equal shotgun/rifle/healing kits, 100 health and 100 shield, 500 wood, 50 elimination siphon and material replenishment.
- Existing walls, floors, ramps, cones, repairs and crosshair edits work with ownership and material limits. Builds, equipment and dropped items reset between rounds.
- One life per round, no friendly fire or knockdowns, automatic spectating, three-second round resets and a closing storm that prevents endless camping.
- Public first-to-five series and private first-to-three/five/seven/ten. Classic and Pressure kits, four bot skill settings, or human-only private rooms.
- Dedicated scoreboard, teammate vitals, countdown sounds, original arena presentation, results and rematches. Public series restart automatically.
- Whole-party matchmaking, late private arrivals joining the next round, authenticated guest edits, and host-transfer recovery of scores, structures and inventory.

Version 6.1.0; protocol 60. Research and implementation rationale: [Box Fights reference](BOX_FIGHTS_REFERENCE.md).
