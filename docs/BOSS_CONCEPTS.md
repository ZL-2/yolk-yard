# Ravel Coast boss roster

Rook and Nyx are implemented in Crowns of the Coast alongside Commandant Voss. All three named boss weapons are Mythic.

| Boss | Appearance and encounter | Location | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Marshal Rook, Breakwater Warden** | A former harbor commander wearing navy armor, a battered diving helmet and orange rescue straps. He patrols a cargo gantry with a shield made from a ship's hatch. Telegraphs a short winch pull, then pushes with a shotgun; shooting his exposed winch pack interrupts the pull. | **Breakwater Docks**, a fortified salvage ship beside the cargo cranes. The ship's hold becomes a small vault. | **Mythic SG-2 Breakwater Breacher**: tight spread, quick reload, slower follow-up fire. **Anchor Winch**: two rechargeable short grapple charges, no damage or automatic enemy tracking. Harbor vault key, shells and metal. |
| **Lieutenant Nyx, Nightglass Scout** | A recon commander in olive armor, a shredded camouflage half-cape and red binocular optics. Moves between lookout platforms, marks her next position with a visible laser, and throws brief smoke before relocating. Smoke blocks her sight too; she never shoots through it. | **Sable Woods**, a repaired fire lookout above a camouflaged field camp, with a locked woodland supply cache below. | **Mythic DMR-18 Nightglass**: clearer optics, accurate first shot and a slightly smaller magazine. **Veil Projector**: three short smoke charges, no invisibility or invulnerability. Woodland cache key, heavy ammo, three minis and wood. |

Both encounters should have readable wind-ups, several approach routes, nearby cover, and sound/visual cues before attacks. Their mobility items should use the existing five inventory slots and be subject to normal cooldown, hold-to-swap and drop rules. Vault rewards should stay compact so they complement Voss rather than dominate the island.
