USER-SUPPLIED FORTNITE AUDIO
Fortnite audio: Epic Games. These existing assets remain shipped:
- audio/boss-voices.mp3: edited from user-supplied
  ScreenRecording_10-04-2026 20-54-45_1.mp4, a Foundation-henchman montage.
  Original montage editor/upload URL is not recorded. Selected, filtered,
  separated with silence and encoded to mono MP3. Clip source offsets are
  in src/boss-voice.js; provenance is in BOSS_AUDIO_REFERENCE.md.
- audio/field-footstep.mp3 and audio/field-birds.mp3: selected from the
  user-supplied Footsteps - Fortnite - QuickSounds.com.mp3. Footstep sample
  is retained in the distribution but current footsteps are synthesized.
- audio/field-elimination.mp3: selected from user-supplied
  Fortnite Death - QuickSounds.com.mp3.
Field extracts are filtered, trimmed and normalized; see
scripts/extract-field-audio.py and docs/MATCH_POLISH_144.md.
The provided records do not establish redistribution permission or identify
all recording editors. Attribution is provenance, not a license grant.

ART & GAMEPLAY REFERENCES
Illustrated menu, news and loading key art: generated with OpenAI image
tools, composed for Ravel Front. Gameplay geometry, material patterns and
world dressing: original procedural game work. Stormspike and Kestrel
announcement cutouts render the exact gameplay models; only the scenic
background is generated illustration. See scripts/render-update-199.mjs.
Royale Prestige: Tempest Talon, Rift Reaver, Solar Dominion and Apex Sovereign
are original procedural game models. Reward and Locker previews render the
exact gameplay meshes. No external model, texture or audio assets were added.
Fortnite / Epic Games is a gameplay and visual reference, including the
classic five-seat Choppa behavior. Kestrel flight constants are independent
tuning; audio is original, and exact proprietary parity is not asserted.
This independent project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games.
